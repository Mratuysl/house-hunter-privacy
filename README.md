# House Hunter – Privacy Policy

**Last updated:** May 2026

## Data Collected
- Email address (for account registration)
- Password (stored securely via Supabase authentication)
- Property listing data you choose to save (address, price, notes, ratings)

## How We Use Your Data
Data is used solely to provide the House Hunter service — saving and syncing property listings across your household.

## Data Storage
All data is stored securely in Supabase. We do not sell or share your data with third parties.
